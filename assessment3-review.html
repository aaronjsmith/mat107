1.

If your monthly gross pay is $4,000, your income tax rate is 25%, and your “affordable debt” is 15%, what can your monthly car payment be if you already have monthly consumer debt of $200 per month?

Affordable car payment: $250 Take-home $3,000 · 15% = $450 affordable · − $200 existing = $250 Excel: =4000*(1-25/100)*0.15-200

2.

Assume you could put $350 monthly into an investment account that will return 9%. How many months would it take you to pay cash for a car costing $18,495.00 (including the sales tax)? Round up to the nearest whole number of months.

45 months PMT $350, APR 9% monthly, FV $18,495 → NPER ≈ 44.68 → round up to 45 Excel: =ROUNDUP(NPER(9%/12,-350,0,18495),0)

3.

What guideline does the lending industry use to determine if you can “afford” debt?

Consumer debt within 15–20% of your net (take-home) income.

4.

Use the following information to determine the maximum amount that Oscar and Abby can afford to pay on monthly consumer debt payments.

  • Oscar and Abby’s gross salary is $6,250 per month
  • 6.5% is deducted for a 401(k)
  • 12% federal income tax
  • 6.2% Social Security
  • 1.4% Medicare
  • 5% state income tax
  • a. 401(k) contribution: $406.25
  • b. Total taxes: $1,537.50 (12%+6.2%+1.4%+5% = 24.6% of $6,250)
  • c. Take-home: $4,306.25
  • d. Maximum consumer credit (20% of take-home): $861.25

Excel: =6250*6.5/100 · =6250*24.6/100 · =6250−401(k)−taxes · =takehome*0.2

5.

When you submit a typical mortgage payment, not all of it goes to paying principal and interest on the loan you have taken out. True or false?

True. Escrow often includes taxes, insurance, and sometimes mortgage insurance (PMI).

6.

You buy a home for $173,271 and put $13,660 down. You borrow the remainder. The mortgage company also charges a 3% loan and closing fee which you must also borrow. The interest rate is 6.5% for 30 years. Calculate the following (monthly payments for the full term).

  • a. Price: $173,271
  • b. Down payment: $13,660
  • c. Amount after down: $159,611
  • d. Loan fee (3%): $4,788.33
  • e. Total financed: $164,399.33
  • f. Monthly payment: $1,039.12
  • g. Total paid back: $374,083.20 ($1,039.12 × 360)
  • h. Cost of credit: $209,683.87 (total paid − total financed)

Excel: financed =(173271-13660)*(1+3/100) · =PMT(6.5%/12,360,-financed) · =PMT*360 · =total-financed

7.

The mortgage on your new home is $195,856 at 6.5% for 30 years. How many months will it take to pay off if you make an extra $65 payment per month? Round to the nearest whole number.

312 months Base PMT ≈ $1,237.94 · with extra ≈ $1,302.94 · n ≈ 311.59 → 312 Excel: =PMT(6.5%/12,360,-195856) then =ROUND(NPER(6.5%/12,-(base+65),195856),0)

9.

You found a car you could afford for $16,445 after trading in your old car. Finance at 9% APR compounded monthly for 8 years.

  • a. Monthly payment: $240.92
  • b. Divide the rate by 12 and multiply periods by 12 because interest is compounded monthly.

10.

What is the present value of $30,130 at 4% received in 11 years compounded quarterly?

$19,447.27 PV = 30130 ÷ (1 + 0.04/4)^(11×4) Excel: =PV(4%/4,11*4,0,-30130)

11.

What does each of the items in a 100/250/50 car insurance policy mean?

$100,000 bodily injury per person / $250,000 bodily injury per accident / $50,000 property damage.

12.

You provide 70% of the family income and your spouse provides 30% and you have no children. Your total income is $180,000 per year. If you expect funeral expense to be $9,000 for each of you, and you have debts totaling $75,000, how much insurance do you need for yourself using the DINK method?

$61,500 DINK: your % of the debts + your funeral = 70% × $75,000 + $9,000 = $52,500 + $9,000 Excel: =9000+75000*70/100

13.

Jack and Julene have five children. Their health insurance pays 81% of medical expenses each year. If one child has an accident costing $6,352 and the rest of the family incurs $2,323, how much will the insurance company pay that year? (Round to the nearest whole number.)

$7,027 Total $8,675 × 81% = $7,026.75 → $7,027 Excel: =ROUND((6352+2323)*81/100,0)

14.

There are 2 people in a car that has an accident caused by a car with 2 people in it. Every person involved sustains injuries that cost $48,220 each. The car damaged by the other car will cost $40,000 to repair. The at-fault car has a 100/300/50 policy.

  • a. Injury payout: $144,660 (3 other injured parties × $48,220; under $100k/person and $300k/accident)
  • b. Property damage: $40,000 (under the $50k limit)

15.

To become financially secure requires that you put your money to work. In order to become financially secure you need to become financially self-reliant. True or false?

True.

16.

What are the characteristics of a share of stock and what does it provide?

Ownership of the company; dividends if paid; growth or loss in stock value; investment risk. Gains or dividends may be subject to income tax.

17.

If you purchase a bond for $900 that has a face value of $1,000 and a coupon rate of 5%, what is your yield?

5.56% Annual coupon $50 ÷ purchase price $900 = 5.56%. As price falls, yield rises. Excel: =(1000*5/100)/900*100

18.

What are the characteristics of bonds and what do they provide you?

Face value with a maturity date; you are a creditor of the issuer; coupon rate and yield (interest ÷ purchase price). Municipal bonds are often exempt from federal income tax.

19.

You put $1,450 into a college fund for your newborn daughter that grows at 6% per year. How much will she have for tuition in 20 years if it is:

  • a. Compounded semi-annually: $4,729.95
  • b. Compounded weekly: $4,810.84

Excel: =FV(6%/2,20*2,0,-1450) · =FV(6%/52,20*52,0,-1450)

22.

Based on what the Study Guide says, what rule-of-thumb is used to determine how much money you need after you retire?

75% of your monthly gross income.

23.

We have been counseled to eliminate consumer debt. Financial experts say you can afford to spend what percentage of your take-home pay on consumer debt?

15–20% of take-home (net, after-tax monthly income).

24.

What are some of the advantages and disadvantages of credit?

Advantages: enjoy goods/services now; benefit if an asset appreciates more than finance charges; credit-card “float” of up to ~50 days.
Disadvantages: you pay interest until the debt is gone; self-reliance is inhibited; you are obligated to someone else; you could lose assets if you don’t pay.

25.

What are primary investments? Alternate investments?

Primary: bank accounts, stocks, bonds, mutual funds.
Alternate: stamps, art, coins, precious metals/stones, real estate, rare collectibles, etc.

26.

When you are incurring quarterly, annual, monthly or daily interest rates, what do you divide the RATE by for each calculation?

  • a. Daily: 365
  • b. Monthly: 12
  • c. Quarterly: 4
  • d. Annually: 1 (do not divide)

27.

Name some types of consumer debt.

Consumer debt: department store credit, auto loans, credit cards, personal loans.
Not typically counted as consumer debt here: home loan (mortgage), tuition, insurance, rent.

28.

What is self-insurance?

You (individual, company, or corporation) assume the risk of loss yourself, and sometimes save to cover losses that could occur.

29.

What items reduce gross income when calculating take-home pay?

Federal income tax, state income tax, city/local income tax, 401(k) contributions, Medicare tax, Social Security, and other payroll deductions.

30.

What are the 5 steps in calculating the “cost of credit”?

  1. Determine the amount you want to borrow.
  2. Calculate the final loan amount (+ fees, processing costs).
  3. Calculate the payment amount.
  4. Calculate the total amount paid back (payment × number of periods).
  5. Cost of credit = total amount paid back − amount borrowed (financed).

31.

What are the 4 lifelong financial principles?

  1. Set financial goals
  2. Budget
  3. Track net worth
  4. Keep financial records
