Compound growth

FormulaExcel
A = P(1 + r)t annual compound =FV(r,t,0,−P)
A = P(1 + r/m)m·t m times / year =FV(r/m,m*t,0,−P)
PV = FV / (1 + r/m)m·t discount a future amount =PV(r/m,m*t,0,−FV)
annuity FV = PMT·((1+i)n−1)/i i = APR/12 · n = months =FV(APR/12,n,−PMT)
months to save (round up) ordinary annuity NPER =ROUNDUP(NPER(i,−PMT,0,FV),0)

m: annual=1 · semi=2 · quarterly=4 · monthly=12 · weekly=52 · daily=365

Rules of thumb

GUIDELINES

  • Consumer debt: 15–20% of take-home
  • Retirement need: 75% of monthly gross
  • Consumer debt: auto / store / cards — not mortgage, tuition, rent, insurance
  • Take-home deductions: taxes, FICA, 401(k), payroll — not groceries

EXCEL FINANCE

  • FV(rate, nper, pmt, [pv])
  • PV(rate, nper, pmt, [fv])
  • PMT(rate, nper, pv, [fv])
  • NPER(rate, pmt, pv, [fv])
  • Sign: −PV or −FV so cash out is positive

Loans & cost of credit

FormulaExcel
financed = (price−down)+fee fee often % of after-down =(price−down)*(1+fee%)
PMT = P·r(1+r)n/((1+r)n−1) r = APR/12 · n = years×12 =PMT(APR/12,n,−P)
total paid = PMT × n 30 yr → × 360 =PMT*n
cost of credit = total − financed =total−financed
extra $ → fewer months round to nearest n =ROUND(NPER(r,−(base+extra),P),0)

5 steps: amount → +fees → payment → total paid → cost = total − financed.

Insurance

FormulaExcel
100/250/50 per person / accident / property =MIN(cost,limit)
insurer pays coinsure% × claims round to nearest $ =ROUND(total*pct,0)
DINK = funeral + (your% × debts) no kids; share of debts =funeral+debts*pct
bond yield = coupon ÷ price coupon = face × rate =(face*rate)/price

LIABILITY PAYOUT

  • Each injury: min(cost, per-person)
  • Then cap accident total
  • Property: min(repair, property limit)
  • Mortgage PMT often includes escrow (tax/ins/PMI)

Principles

  1. Pay the Lord first
  2. Pay yourself second
  3. Spend less than you earn
  4. Save and invest the difference
  5. Collect interest — don’t pay it
  6. Will, desire, and discipline

Lifelong: goals · budget · net worth · records.

Primary: bank, stocks, bonds, funds. Alt: art, coins, metals, real estate.

Stock = ownership (div/gain/risk). Bond = creditor, coupon, maturity.
